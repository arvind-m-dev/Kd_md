   We use a **30-day billing cycle**, counted from the date you first subscribe.

   - **10 June** — Himanshu subscribes to 1 city + 1 asset class. His next billing date is **10 July** (30 days later).

   - **9 August onwards** — Both cities are billed together as part of his regular monthly cycle.

   The same logic applies when adding a new asset class.

   **In short:** Existing subscriptions renew on schedule. New additions are prorated to align with your next billing date.
