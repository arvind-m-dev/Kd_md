### Content
#### Business Rules

**1. Onboarding, Registration, & Entry Rules**
- **Entry Point Convergence**: All users arriving via Social Media CTAs (such as LinkedIn or Meta ads) or through the direct website URL (Keydemand.in) must converge on the exact same registration URL.
- **Mandatory Registration Fields**: The onboarding flow requires capturing user details (Name, and mobile number verified via OTP) alongside a single active demand (capturing Asset class, transaction type, city, location, Carpet area, and Floor).
- **T&C Consent**: Registration is gated by a mandatory clickable tick-box to accept the platform's Terms and Conditions.
- **Initial Free Posting Quota**: Upon initial registration, 1 demand is added to the brand's account. Users can subsequently add up to 2 more demands, allowing for a total of **3 free demands**.
- **Enterprise Integration Trigger**: If a registering user selects the "Enquire for Enterprise Solution" option during onboarding, the lead must be instantly transferred to the internal Keydemand team.
- **Retail Microsite Redirection**: For users acquired specifically via social media CTAs, the post-registration landing page must redirect them to a city-specific **Microsite for Retail**. This microsite dynamically displays listing relevancy, a supply summary, and an option to explore other property options.

**2. Brand Posting Restrictions & Asset Locks**
- **Asset Posting Restrictions**: **Retail Brands** can post **only Retail demands**. They are strictly prohibited from posting other asset classes (such as F&B or Office). However, they are permitted to browse and view property cards outside of their asset class under the global "Explore Properties" header navigation. They can explore Retail, F&B and Office.
- **Post Caps**: **Retail Brands** are permitted to add further **Retail** demands *only* after they are officially onboarded as enterprise users (which is triggered once **3 demands** have been successfully posted).
- **Mandatory Brand Identity**: When a brand posts a demand, their official **company or brand name must be clearly displayed** on the listing.
- **Brand Logo and Card Display States**:
  - If a brand profile image has been uploaded by the user, it must display directly on their demand card.
  - If no brand logo is provided, the system must automatically select a relevant placeholder image based on the chosen asset class.

**3. Account Lifecycle (Deactivation & Deletion)**
- **Manual Account Deletion Flow**:
  - Brands submit deletion requests by filling out a designated Form located under the Profile section.
  - Upon receipt of the form, the Keydemand team must manually reach out to the user to finalize account deletion.
  - **Inventory Impact**: Deleting a profile results in the **permanent deletion** of all associated inventory.
- **Account Deactivation Flow**:
  - Brands can deactivate their accounts instantly by clicking the "Deactivate Account" button under their profile, which triggers a confirmation dialog box.
  - Deactivating the account pauses all activity: the user is marked inactive, and **all active listings are hidden** from public view on the platform.
  - **Inventory Impact**: Deactivating a profile **pauses and deactivates** associated inventory.
  - **Reactivation**: A deactivated brand can return and reactivate at any time via a **"Reactivate account"** button on their homepage.

**4. Live Brand Dashboard & Relevancy Matching**
- **Real-time Dashboard Metrics**: The home page must link to a live, impromptu dashboard displaying:
  - **Demand Listings**: A summary of the total number of the **Retail Brand**'s own demands across all asset classes, ordered newest first.
  - **Property Relevancy**: Total count of matching properties presented to the brand's demands.
  - **Leads/Enquiries**: Captures total phone number requests received.
  - **Enquiries Sent**: Captures contacts viewed, relevant projects, and outbound image requests.
- **My Demands Filters**: Under "My Demands", brands must be able to search and sort using:
  - *Filters*: Search by PropertyId, title, location, Asset Class, City, and Status (Active/Deactivated).
  - *Sorting*: Sort by newest first, most leads, and recent matches.
  - *Visual Priority*: Newest and unopened leads must be displayed first as cards.
- **Relevancy Sorting Priority**: Listings with active relevancy matches are automatically prioritized, pushed to the top of the feed, and flagged with a **red dot notification**.
- **Dynamic Relevancy Alerts**: Any new matched relevancy triggers a red dot over the dashboard counter. Clicking this tab automatically displays the latest matches at the top of the screen, highlighted with the red dot.
- **Leads Navigation**: Clicking on the dashboard "Leads" metric takes the brand directly to the Leads page, displaying open demands that have received leads (ordered newest first, displaying specific lead counts per location).

**5. Inbound & Outbound Phone Number Requests**
- **Outbound Contact Reveal Logic (Brand viewing Property)**:
  - The property supplier's contact details are gated behind a Call-to-Action (CTA).
  - When a logged-in Brand user clicks to view a property's contact number, **both parties gain access to each other's contact information**.
  - The contact details are shared over WhatsApp with the brand.
  - The property owner/lister instantly receives an in-app lead notification and a WhatsApp alert containing the **Brand Name, User Type, Mobile Number, and Property Viewed**.
  - The lead is marked **"Unread"** on the lister's dashboard until they open the details.
- **Inbound Contact Requests Received (Agents/Admins viewing Brand)**:
  - Tracks the number of phone numbers requested on the brand's listings.
  - *Fields captured*: Name, Mobile Number, Property/Demand/Project ID, User-Type, Timestamp, and Accept/Reject buttons.
- **Brand-Only Accept/Reject Actions**:
  - The manual Accept/Reject options on contact requests apply **strictly to Brands** (as their demand cards feature a "Request Contact" CTA).
  - **SLA Window**: The brand has **4 working days** (excluding Saturdays and Sundays) to accept or reject the contact request.
  - **72-Hour Expiry**: If a request remains unanswered after **72 hours**, it is automatically dropped, nullified, and moved to "Rejected" status.
- **Outbound System Notifications (Sent to Requesting User)**:
  - *Approved*: "Your request to view phone number has been approved, kindly connect with them on \<number\>".
  - *Rejected*: "Your request to view the number and connect has been disallowed".
  - *Nullified/Expired*: "Your request to view number and connect has been unanswered and thus dropped".

**6. Outbound Image Requests**
- **Image Request Tracker**: Outbound image requests sent by the **Retail Brand** to see similar properties must be tracked on a dedicated dashboard table.
- **Required Table Fields**: Project/Property ID (clickable and redirectable to the listing page), Asset Class, City, Location, Transaction Type, Posted By, and Date Requested.
- **Image Request Status Displays**:
  - **Accepted**: Shows the "Accepted" label with a **"View Images"** CTA (displays the property listing with images revealed).
  - **Pending**: Shows the "Pending" label (images remain locked).
  - **Rejected**: Shows the "Rejected" label with a **"View Listing without Images"** CTA (displays the listing without images).

**7. Dashboard Slider Interactions**
All dashboard summary metrics must open detailed sliding side panels according to this matrix:
- **Active Demand Card**: Clicking this scrolls the page down to the demands section.
- **Relevancy**: Clicking this scrolls the page down to the My Demands section.
- **Leads Received**: To see all leads, click "Leads Received." The slider will open and display all leads. When clicked on number of leads for demands, then it opens 2 tabs. It consists of phone number viewed, contact requests. **Phone number viewed** is on one tab where the slider will open and display the numbers viewed. and **Contact requests** received are on another. You can choose to accept or reject contact requests.
- **Enquiries Card**: To see the list of enquiries received, click "Enquiries Card." The slider will open and display all the enquiries with three tabs. **Projects Enquired:** To see a list of projects enquired, click "Projects Enquired." The slider will open and display the projects enquired, **Numbers Viewed:** It will open numbers viewed of agent, keydemand team and tenant, **Images Requested**: To see a list of images requested, click "Images Requested." The slider will open and display the images requested.
- **Your Listing - Demands**: To see the full list of your demands, click "Your Listing - Demands." The slider will open and display the list of demands.

**8. Interactive Projects & Callback Options**
- **Project Interest Inquiry**: Brands can show interest in Premium Projects, Exclusive Projects, or general city projects by clicking "Show Interest". This triggers a pre-filled form pulling details from their profile: **Name, Mobile, Email, and User Type**. Clicking submit displays the success message: "Thank you for showing interest".
- **Popular Cities Discovery**: Clicking "Explore" on popular city cards opens all properties and projects matching the chosen city.
- **Low-Abandonment Callback Button**: The "Request a Callback" button in the About section is designed to capture **only the mobile number as mandatory** to prevent form abandonment; name and email fields must be optional.
- **Calendly Booking Integration**: Submitting the callback form opens a specific Calendly link, allowing the brand user to book a slot for a live representative demo.

**9. Alerts, Notifications, & Support**
- **WhatsApp Notification Settings**: A toggle button under settings allows brands to choose whether they wish to receive notifications.
- **Notification Block Restriction**: Only promotional messages can be stopped; brands are strictly **blocked from disabling transactional WhatsApp messages** (e.g., matching relevancies, leads, interest received, or phone number enquiries).
- **Automated Confirmation Alerts**: When a brand adds a new demand, the platform automatically triggers a confirmation WhatsApp message to themselves containing the location details and a PDF download link of their listing.
- **Demand Active Status Alert**: An automated system check occurs **once every 3 months** to verify if the brand's demand is still active.
- **Help Desk**: Standard support is channeled via contact@keydemand.in along with a self-help chatbot utilizing standard website FAQs.

## Version 1.0 Brand- F&B Home Page - Specific Page  [AUTHORITATIVE]

### Content
#### Business Rules

**1. Onboarding, Registration, & Entry Rules**
- **Entry Point Convergence**: All users arriving via Social Media CTAs (LinkedIn or Meta ads) or through the direct website URL (Keydemand.in) must converge on the exact same registration URL.
- **Mandatory Registration Fields**: The onboarding flow requires capturing user details (Name, mobile number verified via OTP) alongside a single active demand (capturing Asset class, transaction type, city, location, Carpet area, and Floor).
- **T&C Consent**: Registration is gated by a mandatory clickable tick-box to accept the platform's Terms and Conditions.
- **Initial Free Posting Quota**: Upon initial registration, 1 demand is added to the brand's account. Users can subsequently add up to 2 more demands, allowing for a total of **3 free demands**.
- **Enterprise Integration Trigger**: If a registering user selects the "Enquire for Enterprise Solution" option during onboarding, the lead must be instantly transferred to the internal Keydemand team.
- **F&B Microsite Redirection**: For users acquired specifically via social media CTAs, the post-registration landing page must redirect them to a city-specific **Microsite for F&B**. This microsite dynamically displays listing relevancy, a supply summary, and an option to explore other property options.

**2. Brand Posting Restrictions & Asset Locks**
- **Asset Posting Restrictions**: Brands can post only demands of F&B. F&B Brands are permitted to add further F&B demands *only* after they are officially onboarded as enterprise users (which is triggered after 3 demands have been posted). They can explore Retail, F&B and Office.
- **Mandatory Brand Identity**: When a brand posts a demand, their official **company or brand name must be clearly displayed** on the listing.
- **Brand Logo and Card Display States**:
  - If a brand profile image has been uploaded, it must display directly on their demand card.
  - If no brand logo is provided, the system must automatically select a relevant placeholder image based on the chosen asset class.

**3. Account Lifecycle (Deactivation & Deletion)**
- **Manual Account Deletion Flow**:
  - Brands submit deletion requests by filling out a designated Form located under the Profile section.
  - Upon receipt of the form, the Keydemand team must manually reach out to the user to finalize account deletion.
  - **Inventory Impact**: Deleting a profile results in the **permanent deletion** of all associated inventory.
- **Account Deactivation Flow**:
  - Brands can deactivate their accounts instantly by clicking the "Deactivate Account" button under their profile, which triggers a confirmation dialog box.
  - Deactivating the account pauses all activity: the user is marked inactive, and **all active listings are hidden** from public view on the platform.
  - **Inventory Impact**: Deactivating a profile **pauses and deactivates** associated inventory.
  - **Reactivation**: A deactivated brand can return and reactivate at any time via a **"Reactivate account"** button on the homepage.

**4. Live Brand Dashboard & Relevancy Matching**
- **Real-time Dashboard Metrics**: The home page must link to a live, impromptu dashboard displaying:
  - **Demand Listings**: A summary of the total number of the brand's own demands across all asset classes, ordered newest first.
  - **Property Relevancy**: Total count of matching properties presented to the brand's demands.
  - **Leads/Enquiries**: Captures total phone number requests received.
  - **Enquiries Sent**: Captures contacts viewed, relevant projects, and outbound image requests.
- **My Demands Filters**: Under "My Demands", brands must be able to search and sort using:
  - *Filters*: Search by PropertyId, title, location, Asset Class, City, and Status (Active/Deactivated).
  - *Sorting*: Sort by newest first, most leads, and recent matches.
  - *Visual Priority*: Newest and unopened leads must be displayed first as cards.
- **Relevancy Sorting Priority**: Listings with active relevancy matches are automatically prioritized, pushed to the top of the feed, and flagged with a **red dot notification**.
- **Dynamic Relevancy Alerts**: Any new matched relevancy triggers a red dot over the dashboard counter. Clicking this tab automatically displays the latest matches at the top of the screen, highlighted with the red dot.
- **Leads Navigation**: Clicking on the dashboard "Leads" metric takes the brand directly to the Leads page, displaying open demands that have received leads (ordered newest first, e.g., showing specific lead counts per location).

**5. Inbound & Outbound Phone Number Requests**
- **Outbound Contact Reveal Logic (Brand viewing Property)**:
  - The property supplier's contact details are gated behind a Call-to-Action (CTA).
  - When a logged-in Brand user clicks to view a property's contact number, **both parties gain access to each other's contact information**.
  - The contact details are shared over WhatsApp with the brand.
  - The property owner/lister instantly receives an in-app lead notification and a WhatsApp alert containing the **Brand Name, User Type, Mobile Number, and Property Viewed**.
  - The lead is marked **"Unread"** on the lister's dashboard until they open the details.
- **Inbound Contact Requests Received (Agents/Admins viewing Brand)**:
  - Tracks the number of phone numbers requested on the brand's listings.
  - *Fields captured*: Name, Mobile Number, Property/Demand/Project ID, User-Type, Timestamp, and Accept/Reject buttons.
- **Brand-Only Accept/Reject Actions**:
  - The manual Accept/Reject options on contact requests apply **strictly to Brands** (as their demand cards feature a "Request Contact" CTA).
  - **SLA Window**: The brand has **4 working days** (excluding Saturdays and Sundays) to accept or reject the contact request.
  - **72-Hour Expiry**: If a request remains unanswered after **72 hours**, it is automatically dropped, nullified, and moved to "Rejected" status.
- **Outbound System Notifications (Sent to Requesting User)**:
  - *Approved*: "Your request to view phone number has been approved, kindly connect with them on \<number\>".
  - *Rejected*: "Your request to view the number and connect has been disallowed".
  - *Nullified/Expired*: "Your request to view number and connect has been unanswered and thus dropped".

**6. Outbound Image Requests**
- **Image Request Tracker**: Outbound image requests sent by the F&B brand to see similar properties must be tracked on a dedicated dashboard table.
- **Required Table Fields**: Project/Property ID (clickable and redirectable to the listing page), Asset Class, City, Location, Transaction Type, Posted By, and Date Requested.

  - **Pending**: Shows the "Pending" label (images remain locked).
  - **Rejected**: Shows the "Rejected" label with a **"View Listing without Images"** CTA (displays the listing without images).

#### Business Rules

**Onboarding, Registration, & Entry Rules**
- **Registration Entry Points**: Both Social Media CTAs and the Direct registration link (Keydemand.in) route users to the exact same registration flow.
- **Mandatory Fields for Office Path**: When a user selects the **"Office Space"** path, the system must capture their **Transaction Type, Company Name, City, Location, Carpet Area, and a mandatory tick-box for T&C acceptance**.
- **Managed Office Lead Routing Checkbox**: The onboarding form contains a prompt asking: *"Also interested in Managed Office?"* alongside an option to *"Enquire for Enterprise Solution"*. If the user checks the managed office box, the inquiry is immediately sent to the Keydemand admin team for offline routing, though their standard office demand is still posted on the platform.
- **Post-Onboarding Microsite**: After registration is complete, the tenant is redirected to a **city-specific Microsite** that displays their listing relevancy, a supply summary, and a call-to-action to explore properties.
- Office Tenants can post Office and Managed Space only if they checked Also interested in Managed Space in initial onboarding form, otherwise they are allowed to post only office space. For Office Tenants, the company/brand name must be displayed when they post a demand.
- Office Tenants can explore both Managed Space and Office properties.

**Demand Limits & High-Value Enterprise Routing**
- **Posting Limits**: Office Tenants are strictly capped at posting **exactly 1 free demand**. To post any additional demands, they must be officially onboarded as an **Enterprise Solution** client.
- **High-Value Demand Routing**: If an Office Tenant submits a demand with a Carpet Area exceeding a city-specific threshold (e.g., **5,000 sq ft in Mumbai**), the system automatically flags it as **"High-Value"**.
  - *Visibility*: High-Value demands are completely hidden from external agents.
  - *Routing*: The lead is automatically routed exclusively to the internal Keydemand team for customized, offline handling.
- **Manual Enterprise Submissions**: Any demand where the user actively selects the "Enterprise Solution" prompt is instantly routed to the Keydemand admin team.

**Contact Sharing & Disclosure Logic**
- **Two-Way Reveal Mechanic**: When an Office Tenant clicks to view a supplier's phone number, the contact details are sent to the tenant via WhatsApp, and the supplier is simultaneously notified. This acts as a **two-way reveal**:
  - The viewer initiates the event passively without requiring an approval action.
  - Once clicked, both parties instantly receive each other's contact details.
  - The property lister/owner is sent a WhatsApp alert containing the **Viewer's Name, User Type, Mobile Number, and the specific listing viewed**.
  - The lead is displayed as **"Unread"** on the lister's dashboard until they actively click it.
- **Contact View Limits**: Non-enterprise Office Tenants are limited to viewing a maximum of **5 supplier contact numbers**. Attempting a 6th view triggers a block and prompts them to connect for the Enterprise Solution.

**Impromptu Live Dashboard & Metrics**
The platform features a live, impromptu dashboard linked directly from the homepage. Because there is no separate inbox, all demand responses, matches, and actions are accessed here.
- **Key Metrics Tracked**:
  - **Total Active Demands**: Displays a summary of the tenant's own demands, capturing the count of relevant properties, projects, and leads received.
  - **Property Relevancy**: The total number of listings matching the active demands.
  - **Leads/Enquiries (Inbound)**: Total count of users who have viewed the tenant's phone number.
  - **Enquiries Sent (Outbound)**: Total count of contact numbers viewed on external listings, alongside images requested and relevant projects.

**Listings Filters, Sorting, & Relevancy Flags**
- **My Demands Field Sorting**: The My Demands section displays the **DemandId, Asset Class, City, and Location**.
- **Card Priority States**: By default, demands are listed in the order of **newest first**, with newest and unopened leads prioritized on card views.
- **Relevancy Priority**: Any demand that has active property matches is automatically prioritized, pushed to the top of the feed, and flagged with a **red dot notification**.
- **Dynamic Relevancy Notifications**: When a new property-to-demand match occurs, a red dot appears over the relevancy count. Clicking this tab automatically displays the latest matching properties at the top of the screen, highlighted with the red dot.
- **Leads Dashboard Navigation**: Clicking the "Leads" metric on the dashboard redirects the tenant directly to their open demands that have active leads, displayed newest first with localized counts (e.g., *"Mahim property has 3 leads"* or *"Andheri property has 1 lead"*).

**Outbound Image Request Management**
When an Office Tenant requests to view hidden images on a property listing, the request and its current status are tracked on a dedicated dashboard table.

#### Business Rules

**1. Posting Permissions & Asset Rules**
- **Authorized Posting Rights**:
  - **Managed Space Operators** are strictly restricted to posting **only Managed Space** listings.
  - If a Managed Space Tenant opts for Managed Space, the requirement is visible only to the Keydemand team. The requirement is not visible to external agents or operators directly
  - Managed Office supply(property) is added ONLY by the Keydemand team and cannot be added by agents.
- **Operator Administrative Workflow**: Managed office operators are treated identically to "Developers" on the platform. They are restricted from logging into the platform directly; instead, Keydemand teams collect all center/hub details offline and manually upload them.
- **Mandatory Company Identity**: The official **company or brand name must be clearly displayed** on the platform whenever they post a demand.
- **Asset Class Structural Nesting**: "Managed Space" is established as a new asset class nested entirely under the **"Projects"** structure rather than standard property inventory.
- **Transaction Parameter Locks**: Transaction options for Managed Space projects are strictly restricted to **"Lease"** (with traditional "Sale" and "Lead" parameters completely removed for this asset class).
- **Registration Field Requirements**: Selecting "Managed Space" as the user type during registration requires capturing: **Mobile number and OTP, Name, Company name, City, Location, and Number of seats**.
- **Onboarding Demand Limits**: Managed Space Tenants can initially add only **1 demand** upon registration. They are strictly blocked from adding further demands unless they are officially onboarded as enterprise users.
- **Consent and Upgrades**: Registration requires mandatory **T&C acceptance**. They have an option to *"Enquire for Enterprise Solution"*.
- **Demand Card Visual States**: If a profile brand image is uploaded by the tenant, it must display directly on their demand card; if not provided, the system auto-selects a placeholder based on the active asset class.

**2. Discovery & Navigation Logic**
- **Cross-Asset Exploration**: Managed Space Tenants are permitted to explore both **Managed Space** and standard **Office** properties.
- **Inventory Classification**: All inventory provided by managed operators (individual centers or coworking hubs) must be classified under **"Projects"** rather than standard individual property inventory.
- **Header Navigation Access**: The logged-in dashboard header features:
  1. **Keydemand Logo** (redirects back to the homepage).
  2. **Explore Properties** (allows users to explore properties uploaded by anyone with only the Managed Space Tenant asset class).
  3. **Post Demand** (restricts posting to properties of Managed Space Tenant).
  4. **Notifications**.
  5. **Enterprise Solution Hamburger (Profile Menu)**.
  6. **Live Dashboard**.
- **Requirement Visibility Lock**: If a Managed Space Tenant opts for Managed Space, their posted requirement is **visible only to the Keydemand team and admin**; it is completely hidden from external agents or operators.
- **Post-Registration Landing (Microsite)**: For users acquired via social media CTAs (LinkedIn or Meta), the post-registration landing page redirects to a city-specific **Microsite**. This microsite dynamically displays a managed supply summary, office/managed space city-specific relevancy, and an option to explore other properties.
- **Search Location Matching**: When demands are matched against properties, the cards are displayed directly below. Clicking "Explore Properties" displays similar properties located in the same geographical area as the user's active demand.
- **Blurred Property Cards**: On the Microsite, property cards are displayed as blurred, featuring a call-to-action button to request photos directly from the listing Agent.

**3. High-Value Routing & Enterprise Limits**
- **Asset Class Distinction**: "Managed Space" is under the **"Projects"** hierarchy to distinguish operator hubs from traditional properties.
- **Lease Enforcement**: All transactions for Managed Space projects must strictly and exclusively support **"Lease"**, removing traditional sale or general lead options.
- **High-Value Redirection**: If a Managed Space Tenant requests a size exceeding a custom, city-specific threshold (e.g., [XYZ sq ft] in a selected city), the system automatically flags the demand and transfers the lead to the Keydemand team for customized, offline handling.
- **Enterprise Features**: Managed office accounts are granted access features similar to those of an Enterprise user.
- **Notification Alerts**: Upon a new registration or demand capture, a system notification entry is logged in the admin central log, and an automated WhatsApp alert is sent directly to the Keydemand regional representative.

**4. Contact Reveal & Lead Tracking**
- **Contact Sharing Hook**: When a Managed Space Tenant clicks the CTA to view contact details, the contact number is shared with them via WhatsApp. Simultaneously, the supplier is notified.
- **Supplier Notification Rules**: The notified supplier will be the internal Keydemand team.
- **Two-Way Reveal Mechanic**: Viewing a phone number is a passive event requiring no approval action. Once clicked, both parties instantly gain access to each other's contact details.
- **Inbound Lister Lead Alerts**: The property owner/lister instantly receives a WhatsApp notification capturing the Viewer's Name, User Type, Mobile Number, and the specific Property/Demand ID viewed. The lead is displayed as **"Unread"** on the lister's dashboard until clicked.
